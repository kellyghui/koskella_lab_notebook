## Results:
- Under increased mortality (dil rate), faster grower is favored (PT23), but increasing mortality from KB/2->KB/20 results in less favor for PT23 by an order of magnitude
- Under increased mortality (KB/20) and competition (50:50), the slower grower (DC3000) prevails

- DC (slower grower) is a better competitor, thriving under stress
- PT (faster grower) is a better grower, doing well under stress until out-competed by the DC (the slower grower)
