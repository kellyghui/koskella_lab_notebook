## Results:
- Under increased mortality (dil rate), faster grower is favored (PT23), but increasing mortality from KB/2->KB/20 results in less favor for PT23 by an order of magnitude
- Under increased mortality (KB/20) and equal competition (50:50), the slower grower (DC3000) prevails

- DC (slower grower) is a better competitor, thriving under stress (its growth is not stunted in coculture)
- PT (faster grower) is a better grower, doing well under stress until out-competed by the DC (its growth is severely stunted in coculture 50:50 - but this could be biased by how high PT23 conc was in monoculture)

- PT23 has a higher resilience to growth from rare in increased mortality conditions (KB/20) when DC is present (1:100), whereas DC dies totally when grown from rare (100:1)
