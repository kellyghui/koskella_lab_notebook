# Protocols

## Passaging (Day 1)

### Materials
1. 96 deep well plate
2. Aero seal
3. P20, P200, P100 microchannel, P1000 microchannel
4. Media reservoirs (1 big for KB, 2 small for culture)

### Plate Set-up
![IMG_6952](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc475240-c97d-453e-b207-e64853ccfdfe)
- 1500 uL KB in every well
- 1470 uL KB + 30 uL culture for 1st two cols
- 1470 uL KB + 30 uL culture + 15 uL phage for last 2 cols

### Procedure
1. Pipette in all the KB
2. Then pipette in the culture
3. Use P20 to pipette in phage one by one (so they don't aerosolize)
4. Use microchannel to mix wells after above steps completed
5. Aero seal the lid
6. Place on shaker

### Passaging (Day 2 on)
- 1500 uL KB in every well
- 1485 uL KB + 15 uL culture passaged from each previous well

### Passage collection (odd passaging days)

### Materials
1. 24 2 mL tubes (glycerol stocks)
2. 12 2 mL tubes (before phage extraction)
3. 12 2 mL tubes (after phage extraction)
4. p1000 + tips
5. 50 % glycerol
6. 2 tube racks
7. 3 square plates

### Procedure
Make glycerol stocks
1. Mix original sample first
2. In each 2 mL tube, pipette 600 uL of 50% glycerol and 600 uL of each sample from 96 deep-well
3. Vortex or mix
4. Freeze

Make Phage extract
1. Pipette 600 uL of each phage mixture into empty tube
2. Go to fume hood
3. 5-6 drops of chloroform into phage tubes (around 100 uL split into 2 tubes)
4. Vortex all
5. Extract 100 uL phage extract from supernatant & put in new tubes
6. Chuck old tubes

Spot check 
1. Square plates in 4 quadrants
2. 300 uL PT23 + SA, swirl (do in middle of collection), sit 10 min
3. Drop 10 uL phage in each quadrant + let sit 10 min
4. Save phage stocks in fridge just in case
