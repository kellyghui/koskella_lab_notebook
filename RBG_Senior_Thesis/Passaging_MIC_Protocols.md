# Protocols

## Passaging (Day 1)

### Materials
1. 96 deep well plate
2. Aero seal
3. P20, P200, P100 microchannel, P1000 microchannel
4. Media reservoirs (1 big for KB, 2 small for culture)

### Plate Set-up
![IMG_6952](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc475240-c97d-453e-b207-e64853ccfdfe)

### Procedure
1. Pipette in all the KB
2. Then pipette in the culture
3. Use P20 to pipette in phage one by one (so they don't aerosolize)
4. Use microchannel to mix wells after above steps completed
5. Aero seal the lid
6. Place on shaker
